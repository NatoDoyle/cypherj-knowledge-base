Self-blame isn’t when you hate yourself.

> **It’s when you stop living in first person for so long you turn into a surveillance system pointed at you.**

Every moment gets reviewed before you’re allowed to feel it.

Every conversation gets replayed.

Every mistake becomes courtroom footage.

Every awkward moment becomes evidence.

And some of you have been doing this for so long…  
you think this is what responsibility feels like.

But it isn’t.

You thought if you monitored yourself hard enough, corrected yourself hard enough, punished yourself hard enough, you could eventually become someone impossible to reject.

Impossible to burden.

Impossible to misunderstand.

Impossible to leave.

But that person does not exist.

So your healing became infinite because you were never actually trying to heal.

You were trying to purify yourself.

Trying to remove every flaw, contradiction, insecurity, mistake, wound, and sharp edge from yourself before allowing yourself freedom.

Before allowing yourself rest.

Before allowing yourself softness.

Before allowing yourself love without suspicion.

And that’s the real cost of self-blame.

It doesn’t just hurt you.

It turns your entire life into a constant inspection process.

Some of you cannot even enjoy good moments without monitoring them while they’re happening.

You hear yourself talking while you talk.

You watch yourself existing while you exist.

You analyze your tone while you’re trying to laugh.

You review your reactions while someone is loving you in real time.

That’s not living.

That’s surviving yourself.

And the terrifying part is some of you genuinely believed the punishment was protecting people from you.

You thought if you stopped blaming yourself, you would become careless.

Selfish.

Dangerous.

So you held yourself at gunpoint internally for years and called it growth.

But listen carefully.

You were never supposed to need fear to become a better person.

Healing was never becoming someone who no longer needed forgiveness.

That person is impossible.

Healing was never becoming someone without contradiction.

That person is impossible.

Healing was never becoming someone who never burdens anyone, never fails, never hurts people, never regresses, never needs grace again.

That person is impossible.

And that’s why you’re exhausted.

Because you’ve been trying to earn freedom from a sentence no human being could ever complete.

You turned your mind into a place nobody could rest in especially you.
