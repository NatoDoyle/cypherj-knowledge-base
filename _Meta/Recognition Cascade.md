# Recognition Cascade

## Summary

The Recognition Cascade is Justin Scott's **unified macro-framework** — self-described as "version 13" of his overall model — an attempt to map, in a single structure, how consciousness builds, breaks, and repairs across personal, relational, and civilizational scales. It rests on a metaphysical substrate shared with the [[Light-Identity Unified Theory]]: awareness is an eternal field, and embodiment is a **sequence** that renders coherent possibility into actualized selfhood through lived encounter — *being is what survives encounter*. From there it stacks five layers, each only as stable as the layer beneath it: **Incidence** (awareness folds and an aperture forms), **Vantage** (recognized truths become viewpoints to perceive *from*), **Contact** (two apertures meet; truth must keep authority over the relationship's continuity), **Non-Substitutability** (something specific gets mapped — a key to a lock), and **Basin** (a field that can metabolize what recognition produces under pressure). The generative cause of failure is [[The False Observer]], whose authority transfer produces the **Perceptual Separation Crisis** (PSC) — the self misread as a separate object that can be found insufficient. PSC propagates into the **Human Continuity Crisis** (HCC: people shaped for adaptation before selfhood) and the **Hyper-Predatory Throughput Attractor** (HTA: civilization-scale extraction that profits from populations it no longer needs to integrate). The terminal condition is **Meaning Death**: not the absence of repair capacity but the absence of *belief* in it — "civilizations do not die when they suffer; they die when they stop believing suffering can produce anything except more suffering." The current civilizational condition is diagnosed as pre-terminal instability. Repair runs opposite to collapse — bottom-up — and its only real intervention is not argument or framework but **encounter**: a single instance of contact that produces undeniable recognition. *The field holds what could be. Sequence reveals what is. Recognition makes it real.*
