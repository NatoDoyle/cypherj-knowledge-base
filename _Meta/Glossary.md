# Glossary

The corpus's invented vocabulary, A–Z. Each term links to the concept page that gives its canonical treatment; follow the concept page's "Defining Essays" for the full original text.

- **Anti-Restructuring** — Behaviors that resist healing, not out of malice but out of inherited survival logic; catalogued for Black men and women with their historical origins. → [[Restructuring]]
- **Aperture** — The fold where the awareness field becomes a located point of view; what forms at the Cascade's first layer. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Authority Transfer** — The moment the self-model is granted more authority than direct experience; the installation event of the False Observer. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Cog** — Parallel system: control through labor — "Control the world through toil." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Collective Entropy** — A society's unwitnessed history stored as hidden pressure that must go somewhere. → [[Entropy]]
- **Consensus Reality / Coordinated Rendering** — Shared rendering mistaken for objective truth; only awareness and patterns truly exist. → [[Light-Identity Unified Theory]]
- **Contact** — Two apertures meeting while truth keeps authority over the relationship's continuity; the thing everyone is actually looking for — one interior finding another fully real. → [[Recognition Cascade]] · [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Convergence Basin** — The field produced by the six axes operating above threshold, able to metabolize what recognition produces under pressure; what people mean by "something that works." → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Convergence Principle** — *Does the field hold?* The third principle of the Relational Architecture. → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Dimensional Transfer Function** — LIUT's reframe of entropy as transfer between dimensions of awareness. → [[Light-Identity Unified Theory]]
- **Entropy** — Unprocessed experience: emotional, historical, and spiritual energy never witnessed, named, or metabolized. → [[Entropy]]
- **Entropy Dumping** — One group offloading its unprocessed guilt, fragility, and violence onto another. → [[Entropy]]
- **Entropy Load Index (ELI)** — Symbolic metric of how much unprocessed collective entropy a system carries (America: ~65%). → [[Entropy]]
- **Extractive Grief** — Pre-labeling the state's conversion of public mourning into justification — name it before they name it looting. → [[Narrative Warfare]]
- **Failure Basins** — The attractors that capture a relationship when the convergence basin drops below threshold: Maintenance, Volatility, Closed Loop, One-Sided Adaptation, Dissolution Field. → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **The Fall** — The Genesis frame: before it, presence without distortion (Hearth); after it, the other eight systems. → [[Proto-Fears]] · [[Parallel Systems]]
- **The False Observer** — The cognitive structure that substitutes the representation of experience for experience itself; reflection that never completes its circuit. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Five Layers** — Incidence, Vantage, Contact, Non-Substitutability, Basin — each only as stable as the layer beneath it. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Flame** — Parallel system: blame and projection — "Blame her. Blame him." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **The Garden We Carry** — The Hearth as memory rather than achievement: the journey is not forward but inward. → [[Proto-Fears]]
- **Hearth** — The ninth system: presence without distortion — the integrated state the other eight bury. → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Hearth Response** — The integrated truth that dissolves each proto-fear. → [[Proto-Fears]]
- **Human Continuity Crisis (HCC)** — The Perceptual Separation Crisis at population scale: people shaped for adaptation before selfhood. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Hyper-Predatory Throughput Attractor (HTA)** — Civilization-scale extraction that profits from populations it no longer needs to integrate; also "Hyper-Throughput Attractor." → [[Recognition Cascade]] · [[Philosophy]]
- **Incidence** — The Cascade's first layer: awareness folds and an aperture forms. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Integration** — Entropy's only resolution: remembering the full story, feeling what was denied, returning to stillness. → [[Entropy]]
- **Keep** — Parallel system: hiding and withdrawal — "Hide yourself." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Light-Identity Unified Theory (LIUT)** — Consciousness as light focused through a metabolic lens; identity as temporary focusing of infinite awareness. → [[Light-Identity Unified Theory]]
- **Love Principle** — *Was the recognition real?* Love as the non-substitutable recognition of a specific person's reality, and the irreversible reorganization that follows. → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Maw** — Parallel system: false image construction — "If you eat this, you'll be like God." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Meaning Death** — The terminal civilizational condition: not the absence of repair capacity but the absence of belief in it. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Metabolic Lens** — The biological body as the focusing instrument of awareness. → [[Light-Identity Unified Theory]]
- **Narrative Command Center** — Counter-narrative infrastructure to be protected as if it were your life's work. → [[Narrative Warfare]]
- **Narrative Warfare** — The first battlefield is the story: brand them before they brand you, or lose without a shot fired. → [[Narrative Warfare]]
- **Non-Substitutability** — The Cascade's fourth layer: something specific gets mapped — a key cut to a specific lock. → [[Recognition Cascade]] · [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Observer-Infrastructure Unit** — The phone: a portable extension of hyper-observer conditions into every gap once reserved for solitude. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Ontological Dying** — Performing living while the capacity for direct participation atrophies from disuse. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Parallel Systems** — The nine survival architectures that emerge when proto-fears go unmetabolized: Maw, Keep, Flame, Thorn, Cog, Reverb, Prism, Pulse, Hearth. → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Perceptual Separation Crisis (PSC)** — The self misread as a separate object that can be found insufficient; the False Observer's first casualty. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Pre-Labeling** — Naming the state's move before it names yours; controlling the rhythm of information. → [[Narrative Warfare]]
- **Presence** — The destination: "Am I?" replaced by "I am" — being that does not require witnessing to be real. → [[Philosophy]] · [[The False Observer]]
- **Prism** — Parallel system: mask-wearing — "Cover yourself. They must not see you." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Proto-Fears** — The eight primordial wounds preceding culture, theology, and personality: Time, Separation, Meaninglessness, Chaos, Being Unseen, Insufficiency, Pain, Loss of Identity. → [[Proto-Fears]]
- **Pulse** — Parallel system: compulsive hunger — "You are now flesh. And flesh is hunger." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Reality Principle** — *Is the system open?* A relationship is alive to the degree that truth retains authority over the structure built to contain it. → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Recognition Cascade** — The unified macro-framework ("version 13"): five layers, the False Observer as root failure mechanism, Meaning Death as terminal state. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
- **Refusal** — Self-erasure's antidote: "I will not purchase belonging with my absence and I will not disappear to keep you." → [[Self-Erasure]]
- **Reintegration** — Returning epistemic authority to direct experience: unwitnessed action, silence without stimulation, genuine play, inhabited grief. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Restructuring** — Dismantling trauma-rooted survival behaviors and rebuilding toward wholeness. → [[Restructuring]]
- **Reverb** — Parallel system: shame loops — "Remember your shame. Never forget." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Role Assignment** — Movement security through distributed roles: protestor, witness, healer, strategist, recorder, protector — no lone heroes. → [[Narrative Warfare]]
- **Self-Erasure** — The adaptive disappearance of identity when survival replaces presence; the point where "Who am I?" stops feeling relevant. → [[Self-Erasure]]
- **Six Axes** — Openness, capacity, mutual legibility, transformation, reciprocity, continuity — multiplicative, not additive; they produce the basin. → [[Relational Architecture]]
- **Thorn** — Parallel system: suffering as identity — "We must suffer now." → [[Parallel Systems]]
- **Unclenching** — Releasing the survival grip: letting the body stand down from a vigilance it no longer needs. → [[Philosophy]] · [[2025-06-07_she-never-got-to-unclench|She Never Got to Unclench]]
- **Unwitnessed Action** — Doing things no one sees, as reintegration practice — experience that does not need an audience to count. → [[The False Observer]]
- **Vantage** — The Cascade's second layer: recognized truths become viewpoints to perceive *from*. → [[Recognition Cascade]]
