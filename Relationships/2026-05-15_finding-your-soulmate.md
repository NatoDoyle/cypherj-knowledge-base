Feel the spark. Follow the signs. Trust the universe. If it’s real, it’ll happen naturally.

And now an entire generation cannot survive emotional friction without believing the relationship itself has become invalid.

> They tell you: *“You’ll know the moment you see them.”*

No you won’t.

You know what you saw? A face. A body. A fantasy. A nervous system reaction. A projection surface. You cannot know a human being at first sight.

But now people think attraction is revelation. A man looked at a woman, felt intensity, and called it destiny. A woman felt emotionally noticed for seven seconds and started building eternity around it. Nobody even asks: *“Can we survive reality together?”*

---

> Then they told you: *“There’s only one.”*

One person. One perfect match. One cosmic assignment.

So now people move through relationships terrified they accidentally ruined fate because they got overwhelmed for a week.

Look at the psychological damage. People are treating love like a disappearing miracle that self-destructs if handled incorrectly.

*“What if I already met my soulmate? What if I ruined it? What if I never feel that again?”*

Meanwhile most people still don’t know how to remain emotionally reachable once fear enters the room.

---

> Then came: *“If it’s meant to be, it’ll be easy.”*

That sentence destroyed people.

Now nobody develops repair. Nobody learns how to stay present through misunderstanding. How to remain open while hurt. How to metabolize irritation without collapsing the bond. How to survive disillusionment without turning the other person into a villain.

The second discomfort appears, and suddenly people think they chose the wrong person—instead of realizing another human being finally stopped feeling imaginary.

---

> Then they told you: *“You complete me.”*

Which sounds beautiful until you realize what people actually mean is: *“Please become the missing architecture of my unstable self.”*

Now relationships are full of people trying to turn another human being into self-esteem, purpose, identity, emotional regulation, proof of worth, proof they’re lovable, proof life isn’t meaningless.

That’s why people suffocate each other now. Nobody is entering love to encounter. They’re entering to stabilize collapse.

---

> Then came twin flames.

The greatest rebrand of emotional inconsistency ever performed.

Now getting ignored for three months is spiritual depth. Someone disappears, returns emotionally unavailable, stares at you intensely, says *“we have a cosmic connection”* — and suddenly your nervous system injury became sacred mythology.

No. You got attached to unpredictability. Your body interpreted inconsistency as significance. That is not divine union. That is emotional destabilization being narrated like destiny.

---

> Then people started saying: *“They’re the love of my life.”*

But never once asked:

*Was I safety in theirs? Could they rest around me? Did my love expand them or manage them? Did I know how to tell the truth? Did I know how to stay reachable? Did I love them—or did I love what they awakened inside me?*

Because some people don’t miss a person. They miss the emotional magnitude of being seen by them.

---

**And underneath all of this is the real catastrophe: people are using destiny to avoid developing relationship capacity.**

That’s it. That’s the whole disaster.

Instead of learning repair, clarity, presence, truthfulness, consistency, mutual responsiveness—people fantasize about a future person who will remove the need for those things entirely.

*“The right person won’t trigger this. My soulmate will just understand. If it’s real, we won’t have to work this hard.”*

So now people are waiting for inevitability because they never learned how to participate in intimacy.

And the worst part? Real pair bonding actually exists. Human beings absolutely can become sacred to each other.

But that sacredness usually emerges after disappointment. After repair. After truth. After staying. After metabolizing reality together. Not before it.

That’s the part nobody tells you. Your soulmate was never supposed to save you from relationship. They were someone you became increasingly real with *through* relationship.

But that’s less marketable than destiny.

So they sold you fate instead of intimacy.
